Protocol

Daily protocol

Core principle

Fix insulin first. Every decision — what you eat, when, how long you fast — is in service of lowering your baseline insulin. Low insulin = fat burning mode. High insulin = fat storage mode. Wheat, sugar, and eating outside your window are the three primary insulin spikes to eliminate.

Morning fast

07:00 — Morning brew + muscle‑sparing movement

Before anything else: 500ml warm water. Then brew: cinnamon + hibiscus + rosemary + ginger + turmeric + black pepper + cayenne.

Movement (non‑negotiable): 10 min skipping + 5 min ab roller + 5 heavy repetitions of squats with the 15kg dumbbells + 5‑10 chest pulls (using the chest expander). This protects muscle tissue from breaking down during long sedentary hours.

During the fast window

Herbal brews, plain water, black coffee only. Hunger is a wave — it peaks at 20 minutes and drops. When it hits: 300ml water + pinch of salt. Do not check phone immediately on waking — this spikes cortisol before your day starts.

Eating window (14:00 – 18:00)

14:00 — ACV shot (15 min before)

1 tbsp apple cider vinegar in 200ml water, 15 minutes before first meal. This is your free Ozempic — it slows gastric emptying and blunts the blood sugar rise from your meal significantly. Do not skip it.

First meal — protein first, always

Always start with protein or fat before any carbohydrate. Eggs, beans, omena, ndengu. Eat slowly. Stop at 80% full.

15:30–16:30 — Optional second meal

Only if genuinely hungry — not bored, not stressed, not habitual. Peanuts + banana, boiled egg. Not mandatory.

Evening

18:00 — Kitchen closes

Brush teeth immediately. Evening brew: ashwagandha + chamomile + cinnamon + hibiscus. Brushing is the most effective behavioural signal to close eating.

22:00 — Wind down

Journal 3 lines. Review tomorrow's one priority task. Every hour of sleep before midnight is worth double.

Dr. Mindy Pelz — cycle-aware additions

Cycle-aware fasting

Days 1–10: ideal for longer fasts (16–24h). Days 11–15 (ovulation): maintain 16:8, don't push longer. Days 16–19: shorter fasts, more digestible protein. Days 20–28: reduce fasting, add more carbs (sweet potato, fruit) — prevents cortisol spike that triggers bingeing before period.

Dr. Huberman additions

Light + timing tools

Outdoor light within 30–60 min of waking — sets your circadian clock, which controls cortisol timing and fat storage. Delay caffeine 90–120 min after waking. Largest meal 4–6 hours before sleep for best metabolic clearance.

12-week plan

Mindset

The only rule that matters

Do not quit on a hard day. Hard days are the protocol working — your body is resisting the change because the change is real. Every hard day you survive makes the next one easier.

On slipping

A slip is one meal. One meal does not break twelve weeks. Log it, note what caused it — stress, boredom, social pressure — and move on immediately. The next meal is clean. There is no punishment fast, no compensation. Just the next meal, clean.

On hunger

Hunger is not an emergency. It is a feeling, and feelings pass. Hunger peaks at 20 minutes then drops whether you feed it or not. The only question is which path you take during those 20 minutes. Drink water. Move to a different room. Do something with your hands.

On non-scale victories

Energy levels. Sleep quality. How your clothes fit. Hunger control. Mood stability. Mental clarity. These change weeks before the scale moves. The scale is the last thing to change and the least reliable indicator of what's actually happening.

On environment

The strongest person in the world cannot out-willpower a kitchen full of the wrong food. Win the environment, not the craving. You make food decisions when you shop, not when you're hungry. Shop right and the hard decision is already made.

Fasting window until 14:00
Morning brew
zero insulin fat burning
Why

Starts your fasted state with thermogenic spices that increase fat oxidation and suppress appetite.

Brew

Cinnamon + hibiscus + rosemary + ginger + turmeric + black pepper + cayenne. Add lemon if available. No milk, no sugar. Boil in batch, store in flask.

Safe during fast

Hibiscus cold-steeped (best fasting drink), cinnamon water (extends fat-burning), ginger water (kills hunger waves), rosemary cold steep (zero insulin), black coffee no milk or sugar.

Avoid during fast

Milk, sugar, baobab in quantity. Chia seeds in water are borderline — can trigger digestive activity.

First meal from 14:00

ACV shot 15 min before eating. Protein or fat goes in first — always.

Eggs + sukuma scramble
high protein low GI
Why

Eggs provide complete protein and healthy fats; sukuma adds fibre and micronutrients with minimal insulin impact.

Ingredients

2 eggs · 1 cup sukuma wiki or spinach · ¼ onion · 1 tomato · ½ tsp turmeric · ½ tsp ginger · pinch cayenne · salt · tiny oil

Method

Fry onion lightly. Add tomato, spices, cook 2 min. Add sukuma, stir 2 min. Push to side, scramble or poach eggs in same pan. Eat slowly.

Sweet potato + egg scramble
balanced complex carb
Why

Cooled sweet potato provides resistant starch; eggs blunt the glucose response.

Ingredients

1 medium sweet potato (skin on) · 2 eggs · ¼ onion · 1 tomato · turmeric · ginger · black pepper · cayenne · ½ tsp moringa

Method

Boil or roast sweet potato. Cool first — cooling increases resistant starch. Dice. Fry onion and tomato with spices. Add sweet potato, stir 2 min. Scramble eggs into same pan. Moringa off heat.

Fermented uji
complex carb gut health
Why

Overnight fermented wimbi flour porridge finished with maziwa mala and cinnamon – the fermentation lowers the glycemic index dramatically, and the probiotics improve gut health. No sugar, no insulin spike.

Ferment (night before)

Mix uji flour with room temperature water — slightly more than cooking ratio. Cover with cloth, not airtight. Leave 12–24 hours. In morning it smells slightly sour. Stir the thin liquid layer back in, do not discard. Cook as normal. Sour taste is mild after cooking.

Finishing

Add maziwa mala after cooling slightly (not boiling — preserves live bacteria). Add cinnamon, moringa, chia seeds, ginger. Very little or no sugar. Optionally mash half a ripe banana in — sweetens naturally.

Ndengu + moringa
very low GI iron + protein
Why

Ndengu (green grams) are a low‑glycemic protein source; moringa adds iron and B vitamins without raising insulin.

Method

Boil ndengu 25–30 min. Fry onion, add tomato and spices (cinnamon, turmeric, ginger, black pepper), cook to paste. Add ndengu and broth, simmer 5 min. Stir moringa off heat — never boil moringa.

Boiled green banana + omena
resistant starch omega-3
Why

Green bananas are rich in resistant starch; omena provides calcium and omega‑3s that reduce inflammation.

Method

Score and peel green bananas under running water (avoids sap staining). Boil in salted water 20–25 min until tender. For omena: rinse well, fry onion, tomato, turmeric, ginger, cayenne. Add omena, stir until done. Serve together.

Sukuma + Spinach + Ndengu Blend
low gas iron rich
Why

Mixed dark greens sautéed in 1 tsp oil with onions and tomatoes, paired with clean green ndengu/yellow beans. Prevents gut sluggishness without cabbage gas – perfect for sensitive stomachs.

Ingredients

1 cup sukuma wiki · 1 cup spinach · ½ cup cooked ndengu or yellow beans · 1 small onion · 1 tomato · 1 tsp oil · turmeric, ginger, black pepper.

Method

Sauté onion and tomato in oil until soft. Add spices, then sukuma and spinach – cook 2‑3 minutes until just wilted. Stir in ndengu, heat through. Eat with a squeeze of lemon.

Wimbi + Egg Sweet Pancake
no oil cinnamon
Why

1 cup millet flour bound with 2 whole kiosk eggs, a mashed small banana, and a heavy teaspoon of cinnamon powder, pan‑fried dry with zero oil to mimic a safe sponge cake – kills sweet cravings without insulin spike.

Ingredients

1 cup wimbi (millet) flour · 2 eggs · 1 small ripe banana (mashed) · 1 tsp cinnamon powder · pinch of salt.

Method

Whisk eggs, then mix in mashed banana, cinnamon and salt. Add wimbi flour slowly to form a thick batter. Heat a non‑stick pan (no oil). Pour small ladles, cook until bubbles form, flip. Serve with a drizzle of maziwa mala if desired.

Cold‑Hack Rice & Gizzards
resistant starch high protein
Why

100g cheap boiled chicken gizzards paired with white rice that has been pre‑cooled for 24 hours – cooling generates resistant starch, cutting active calories by ~50% and blunting the insulin response.

Ingredients

100g chicken gizzards (boiled) · 1 cup cooked white rice (cooled 24h in fridge) · onion, tomato, spices.

Method

Boil gizzards until tender (30‑40 min). Fry onion and tomato, add gizzards. Meanwhile, cook rice a day ahead and refrigerate uncovered. Reheat the gizzards, serve over cold rice (or reheat rice gently – resistant starch remains).

Avocado + Banana (or Cinnamon Wimbi Pancake)
slow carb healthy fat
Why

Half a fresh whole avocado paired with a small banana or a cinnamon‑wimbi pancake. The healthy fats from avocado slow down glucose absorption from the banana, preventing a blood sugar spike and keeping you satiated for hours.

How to eat

Cut half an avocado, slice a small banana. Eat together, or mash the avocado onto the pancake. Ideal as a pre‑workout or afternoon fuel.

Omena + cabbage
omega-3 calcium
Method

Rinse omena well. Fry onion and tomato with turmeric, ginger, cayenne. Add omena. Add shredded cabbage last, cook 3 min only — keep the crunch. Serve as-is or with ¼ cup rice max.

Rice + beans + cabbage (one-pot)
moderate filling
One-pot method

Fry onion, garlic, tomato, cinnamon, ginger, turmeric. Add rinsed rice, stir to coat. Add pre-cooked beans and water at rice ratio. Cook until almost done. Lay shredded cabbage on top, cover, steam 5 min.

Eating order

Eat bean-cabbage portion first, rice second. Njahi (black beans) has the lowest GI — prioritise njahi over red or yellow beans.

Rice + gizzard + beans
very high protein low fat
Method

Boil gizzards 30–40 min until tender — do not rush. Fry onion, tomato, ginger, turmeric, garlic, black pepper, paprika, cayenne. Add gizzards, fry until browned. Add pre-cooked beans + small water, simmer 10 min. Small rice, large sukuma/cabbage on the side.

Sweet potato + ndengu stew
very low GI
Method

Cook ndengu until soft. Boil sweet potato chunks until just tender — do not overcook. Fry onion, tomato, cinnamon, ginger, turmeric, black pepper. Add ndengu with broth. Add sweet potato last, simmer 5 min only. Moringa off heat.

Green banana + bean stew
resistant starch gut health
Method

Add boiled green banana chunks to bean stew in the last 10 minutes of cooking. They absorb the broth and spices. Season beans with cinnamon, ginger, turmeric, black pepper. The resistant starch feeds gut bacteria that reduce fat storage over time.

Afternoon snackoptional
Cold sweet potato + peanuts
low GI fat-loss snack
Why cold

Boil sweet potatoes, cool completely, store. Eat cold with a handful of peanuts. Cold potato has meaningfully lower GI than hot. The peanuts add fat and protein that slow glucose absorption further. This is a legitimate fat-loss snack.

Ripe banana + peanut + cinnamon
craving swap
The combination

1 ripe banana + 1 tbsp peanuts + pinch cinnamon. Eat this when a sweet craving hits. Peanut fat slows the banana sugar. Cinnamon caps the insulin response. Use this instead of chapati or biscuits.

Chapati / cake — harm reduction
harm reduction
Before eating

Eat protein or fat first — two boiled eggs, peanuts, or beans. ACV in water 15 min before if you know it is coming.

After eating

10-minute walk. This brings the blood glucose peak down significantly. Research-backed, not guesswork.

Biscuits rule

After a meal, never on empty stomach. Two biscuits after a protein meal is metabolically very different from two biscuits at 10pm fasted.

Dinner — last mealby 18:00

Kitchen closes at 18:00. Lighter than first meal. No rice at dinner from week 3.

Spinach + egg soup
light no starch
Method

Fry onion and garlic. Add tomato and spices, cook 2 min. Add 1 cup water, bring to simmer. Add 2 cups spinach or sukuma, cook 2 min. Crack 2 eggs directly into simmering broth, poach 3–4 min. No rice or ugali at dinner from week 3.

Beans + potato stew (done properly)
moderate
Method

Cook beans with onion, tomato, cinnamon, ginger, turmeric, black pepper, and generous cabbage or sukuma. Add potato chunks in last 15 min. Eat the bean-vegetable portion heavily, potato lightly. If you cool and reheat the stew, potato GI drops substantially.

Cold potato salad variation

Boil potatoes, cool completely. Toss cold with raw sukuma, diced tomato, onion, lemon juice, black pepper, salt. No mayonnaise. Cooling is what makes this a different food metabolically.

Chicken + beans (small rice)
high protein
Method

Brown chicken in onion, tomato, garlic, ginger, paprika, turmeric, cinnamon, black pepper. Add small water, braise until cooked through. Add beans last 10 min to absorb flavour. More vegetables than rice.

Sweet Cinnamon Omelette (30‑Shilling Cake)
evening snack cinnamon
Why

2 or 3 medium eggs whisked frothy with a heavy dash of cinnamon powder and dry pan‑fried – kills evening emotional snacking for under 30 shillings, provides protein and cinnamon’s insulin‑sensitising effect.

Ingredients

2‑3 eggs · 1 tsp cinnamon powder · pinch of salt (optional).

Method

Whisk eggs vigorously until frothy. Stir in cinnamon. Heat a non‑stick pan (no oil). Pour egg mixture, swirl to spread. Cook until set, flip briefly. Eat like a flat cake.

Environment & Emergency Protocols

🚨 Out All Day – Zero‑Money OMAD

When: Stuck away from home with no food access.

Protocol:
→ Shift to a 24‑hour fast (skip eating entirely).
→ At 11:00 AM and 4:00 PM, place a pinch of raw salt on your tongue followed by plain water – this turns off stomach acid spasms and false hunger.
→ When you return home at night, break the fast strictly with 2 boiled eggs first to shield your stomach wall before any dense food.
→ Wait 15 minutes, then eat your normal meal (protein first).

⚡ Controlled Metabolic Shock (Every 2 Weeks Only)

Strict rules for handling heavy cravings safely:

  1. Never eat them on an empty stomach.
  2. Take an ACV shot (1 tbsp in 200ml water) 15 minutes prior.
  3. Eat 2 boiled eggs or a handful of ndengu first – this acts as a protein shield, trapping incoming wheat sugars.
  4. Wake up at 7:00 AM the next morning and execute 10 heavy squats with the 15kg dumbbells (or 20 bodyweight squats) – this flushes stored glycogen out of muscle cells, mitigating fat storage.

⚠️ Only for once‑every‑two‑weeks occasions. Not a daily rule.

Standard Environment Mode Strategies

Strategies for communal food settings where you cannot control what is served.

11:00 — Tea + mandazi brunch

Strategy

  • Push to as late as possible — 11:30 or noon is better
  • Mandazi: maximum 1. Eat slowly, water between bites
  • Bring peanuts or a boiled egg — eat before the mandazi
  • Black tea over milky sweet tea at this point
Lunch — rice/ugali/chapati + stew

Order of eating

  • Step 1: Eat all vegetables and protein on your plate first
  • Step 2: Eat half the starch — then pause 5 minutes
  • Step 3: If still hungry, continue. If not, stop.
  • Ugali > rice > chapati for GI impact
  • Beans/ndengu/githeri stew is better than meat stew for insulin

What to bring from home

  • Peanuts in a small bag — eat before any communal starch meal
  • Hard‑boiled eggs — eat before rice or ugali
  • ACV in a small bottle — take before lunch
  • Chia seeds — add to any drink

Herb stack

Your herb stack

Cinnamon + ACV lower blood glucose mechanistically. Hibiscus reduces visceral fat. Ginger and cayenne increase thermogenesis. Ashwagandha lowers cortisol measurably in RCTs. You have most of what you need.

Morning brew
Cinnamon
Lowers fasting blood sugar by mimicking insulin at the receptor. Most important herb. ½ tsp per cup. Non-negotiable.
Hibiscus
Anthocyanins reduce visceral fat accumulation and lower blood pressure. Studies show measurable waist circumference reduction. Keep in every brew.
Rosemary
Improves glucose metabolism, mild diuretic (reduces water retention).
Ginger
Thermogenic, reduces appetite, anti-nausea during fasting. Add every morning.
Turmeric
Curcumin reduces systemic inflammation driving insulin resistance. MUST be paired with black pepper — increases absorption 2000x.
Black pepper
Activates curcumin in turmeric. Also mildly thermogenic. Never use turmeric without it.
Cayenne
Capsaicin suppresses appetite and increases fat oxidation for 2–3 hours. Best fasted in the morning.
Evening brew
Ashwagandha
Proven cortisol reduction in multiple RCTs. High cortisol is a primary driver of belly fat. Take at night — wasted in the morning. ½ tsp in brew or as capsule.
Chamomile
Improves sleep quality and lowers post-meal blood sugar. Better sleep = lower ghrelin = easier fasting next morning. Make it strong.
Add to food (off heat)
Moringa
Complete protein, high iron and B vitamins. Always add after cooking — heat destroys nutrients. ½ tsp daily minimum.
Baobab
Very high fibre and vitamin C. Feeds gut bacteria that regulate fat metabolism. Slightly sour, pleasant. Add to uji or stir into water.
Paprika
Mild capsaicin, antioxidants. Use generously in stews — makes food satisfying without extra calories.
Use as gel / drink
Chia seeds
Soak 1 tsp in water 10 min — forms a gel that slows glucose absorption. Add to uji or drink before a meal. Suppresses appetite for hours.
Use carefully
Activated charcoal
NOT daily. Absorbs nutrients and medications. Use only after a genuine binge — ½ tsp in water, then stop.
Sodium bicarb
½ tsp in water for acid relief only. Do not take before eating.

NEAT advantage

Non-Exercise Activity Thermogenesis — calories burned from daily movement — can match a gym session. Maximise what you are already doing.

Maximise daily movement

  • Take the longest walking route whenever possible
  • Stairs over any lift, always
  • Stand rather than sit when you can choose
  • A 10-minute walk immediately after eating lowers blood sugar by up to 30%
  • Carry things rather than asking for help

Micro-exercise anywhere

  • 3 sets of 15 squats before dressing in the morning
  • 20 push-ups before bed
  • Wall sit 3×30s while waiting for anything
  • Calf raises while standing in queues

Bodyweight only — full session

Push-ups, squats, lunges, glute bridges, mountain climbers, plank. 3 rounds of each for 10–15 reps. 20 minutes total. No equipment. No space beyond a mat.
